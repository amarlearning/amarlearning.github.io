I'm a senior data engineer at Citi in Bengaluru, with 8+ years of building and fixing data systems. Most of my days go into Spark. I'm usually the one who gets pulled in when a job is slow, expensive, or failing every night, and I've cut resource usage on some of those by up to 90%. Then I build the tooling so nobody has to dig through those logs by hand again.

Before Citi, I spent four years at Sahaj Software owning terabyte-scale ad-tech pipelines on AWS EMR and Airflow, and before that, three years at TCS building real-time pricing APIs for Cummins.

Outside work, I've contributed to [Apache Airflow](https://airflow.apache.org/), was a maintainer at [CRI-O](https://cri-o.io/) and [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com), and did [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) with TEAMMATES at NUS. When a problem annoys me enough, I turn it into a tool, like [spot-optimizer](https://github.com/amarlearning/spot-optimizer) (11k+ downloads) or [uv-bundler](https://github.com/amarlearning/uv-bundler), and I [write](/blog/) about Spark internals, databases, and functional programming.

Always happy to talk data, open source, or side projects. Say hello on [GitHub](https://github.com/amarlearning), [Medium](https://medium.com/@amarlearning), or [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/amarlearning).
