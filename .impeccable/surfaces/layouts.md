# Site-wide surface brief

Scope: every route of amarpandey.me (home, about, projects, talks, blog list, posts). Visitor mode: Read. Content, links and data stay exactly as written; this brief governs look and feel only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working engineer's notebook that stays out of the way: one narrow column, one crisp sans, white paper, warm stone at night. It refuses the bearblog default and the cream-and-serif editorial costume the site wore before.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white paper with neutrals tinted a hair toward stone; ink near-black, prose a step lighter, meta a muted grey. Geist at 430 for everything, weight steps instead of size jumps. Cards are 12px radius, a hairline border, a faintly darker fill. The only colour is the Litmus strip's green, spent on focus, selection and the Litmus mark itself.

STORY: Who Amar is in one paragraph, what he is building now (Litmus), the work, the writing. The visitor reads, then follows a link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Avatar, name and handle top left, theme toggle top right, quiet nav below; then the intro at 17px filling the column. Litmus begins just under the fold on a laptop.

FORM: Kamran-style single column (owner's pinned reference, position 1 of 1). seed: none (unavailable)

SIGNATURE: The theme toggle crossfades the whole page through a view transition; the Litmus card carries the product's own strip mark.
