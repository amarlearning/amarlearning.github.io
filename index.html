I’m a senior data engineer at Citi in Bengaluru, with 8+ years of building and fixing data systems.

  1. TCS 3 yrs Real-time pricing APIs for Cummins
  2. Sahaj Software 4 yrs Terabyte-scale ad-tech pipelines
  3. Citi now Spark and data platforms

Most of my days go into Spark, fixing jobs that are slow, expensive, or failing every night. I’ve cut resource usage on some by up to 90%, then built tooling so nobody digs through those logs by hand again.

Outside work, I’ve contributed to Apache AirflowContributor, maintained CRI-OMaintainer and DuckDuckGoMaintainer, and did Google Summer of CodeStudent with TEAMMATES at NUS.

When a problem annoys me enough, I turn it into a tool, like spot-optimizer (11k+ downloads) or uv-bundler, and I write about Spark internals, databases, and functional programming.

Always happy to talk data, open source, or side projects.

Litmus

System design practice that judges your decisions, not your diagram. Each problem gives you real requirements and an empty canvas. Every box you add gets a verdict: right-sized, overkill, or about to break, with the reason why.
